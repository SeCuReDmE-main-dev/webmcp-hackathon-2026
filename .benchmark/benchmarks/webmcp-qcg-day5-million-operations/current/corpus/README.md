# Frozen textual corpus

The corpus is intentionally textual and synthetic. It gives the preparation helper stable inputs for ten profile declarations and five governance decisions without executing a quantum SDK, importing a notebook, reading a URL or contacting a provider. Each item is UTF-8 and below the 128 KiB intake limit.

The `.txt` suffix is deliberate: the benchmark describes the source family and expected extension in the manifest, while the fixture remains inert documentation. Q# and OpenQASM are executable product profiles; the other eight profiles remain static-inspection-only.
