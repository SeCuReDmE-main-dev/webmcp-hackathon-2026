DECISION decision-02-gemini-relay
question: How does native Gemini DevTools participate?
accepted_rule: QCG exports a sanitized handoff for human copy/paste and preview/import; a separate Gemini MCP client may target the same page.
evidence_boundary: Chrome documents a user-facing Gemini surface and DevTools MCP, not an extension API that writes the native conversation.
benchmark_assertion: A fixture labelled Gemini is not a live Gemini exchange.
