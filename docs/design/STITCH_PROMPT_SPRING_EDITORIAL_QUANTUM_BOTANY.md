## Product truth

Design **WebMCP-QCG — WebMCP Quantum Call Gate**, a browser-native human-in-the-loop preflight workbench. It inspects a known Q# artifact, evaluates one bounded recommendation, optionally runs one fixed local Q# Bell fixture after a visible one-time consent, and exports a reproducible receipt. Keep provider credentials, paid calls, QPU submission, arbitrary quantum-code execution and scientific interpretation outside the product boundary.

Show `Decide before quantum execution.` and `Working browser prototype`. Keep the external stage labelled `human controlled`. Do not invent metrics, signatures, provider/QPU capacity, network telemetry, an `Execute` button or a fifth tool.

## Spring art direction

Create an asymmetric editorial instrument on a pale mineral surface. Build a central gate chamber with two quiet rails and a blue-cyan seam. Frame it with a detailed, physically plausible deciduous tree: tapered trunk, believable bark fissures, asymmetrical primary and secondary branching, real branch collars, fresh leaf clusters on twigs, rain-dark bark and damp stone. Use botanical depth, contact shadow, reflected light and a few foreground leaves. Every branch must connect structurally; avoid mirror symmetry and floating stems.

Integrate circuit/provenance linework into the tree with restraint. Let a few copper, cyan and soft-gold hairlines follow real branch junctions and resolve into labelled evidence nodes: `human`, `webmcp`, `worker`, `export`. Place a small wireframe evidence cube beside the gate seam to suggest a receipt digest. Show the textual flow `artifact → target snapshot → recommendation → human choice → receipt`. The botanical scene guides attention; it does not encode data by leaf count or glow.

Use fresh cyan, leaf green, restrained warm gold, mist blue and mineral ivory. Keep controls on opaque surfaces with AA contrast. Use layered 1 px borders and shallow shadows; preserve the high-legibility sans and restrained monospace identifiers from the existing contract. Keep a calm reading column and do not cover the page with a photo.

## Four seasons, one behavior

Deliver the same frames and information hierarchy for four themes:

1. `Autumn — provenance`: mature copper foliage, plum/graphite surfaces, rust and amber lines.
2. `Winter — clarity`: bare snow-weighted branches, ice/cobalt surfaces and frosted-gold lines.
3. `Spring — emergence`: fresh leaves, wet bark, mineral/cyan/leaf-green palette; this is the primary frame.
4. `Summer — signal`: full canopy and warm light over QCG navy/electric-blue/cyan/gold.

These are presentation tokens, not product modes. Do not create dark, light, premium or DevTools themes. Do not make the tree a clickable control.
