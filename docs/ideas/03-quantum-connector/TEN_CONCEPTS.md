# Ten WebMCP Quantum concepts

Status: `ACTIVE_RESEARCH`  
Provisional favorite: `1 + 3 + 9`

| # | Concept | Independent promise | Hackathon role |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Quantum Call Gate | Decide whether to execute, simulate, recompile, reuse or reject. | Candidate core |
| 2 | Four-Surface Circuit Concierge | Select Qiskit, TorchQuantum, TFQ or Azure from explicit requirements. | Planning-only alternative |
| 3 | Fermion-to-Qubit Compilation Clinic | Compare structure-aware fermionic compilation with an early mapping path. | Candidate scientific demo |
| 4 | Quantum Report Deduplicator | Reuse evidence when code, circuit, parameters, seed and environment match. | Strong extension |
| 5 | Simulator-Before-QPU Guardian | Stop the workflow as soon as a lower-cost proof answers the question. | Safety alternative |
| 6 | Cross-Framework Experiment Auditor | Identify comparable and non-comparable dimensions across frameworks. | Research tool |
| 7 | Quantum Capability Router | Expose only relevant, currently supported capabilities to the agent. | WebMCP optimization |
| 8 | Budget-Aware Quantum Planner | Compare observable compute, provider and estimation costs. | Later extension |
| 9 | Reproducible Quantum Evidence Pack | Export manifest, versions, hashes, circuits, results and limitations. | Candidate evidence layer |
| 10 | Browser Quantum Lab Notebook | Unite build, compile, execute, visualize and analyze. | Product vision, too broad for first build |

## Provisional combination

### 1 — Decision core

`Quantum Call Gate` supplies the unique product behavior: preventing an unnecessary or invalid call.

### 3 — Scientific case

`Fermion-to-Qubit Compilation Clinic` gives the decision engine a non-trivial, current and source-grounded workflow through `qiskit-fermions`.

### 9 — Proof

`Reproducible Quantum Evidence Pack` ensures that an avoided call is not merely asserted. The report must expose the experiment signature, the validation path, the evidence already obtained and the reason further execution was unnecessary.

## Decision remains open

Today's research may change this combination. Each concept will be scored independently before implementation.
