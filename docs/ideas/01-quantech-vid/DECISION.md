# Quantech Vid decision

Decision date: 2026-08-27  
Status: `REJECTED_FOR_THIS_HACKATHON`

## What was considered

Expose Quantech Vid capture, narration, rendering and evidence operations as WebMCP tools.

## Why it was not selected

- The application is already a functioning local-first studio.
- WebMCP would improve agent access, but would not be essential to its central value.
- The hackathon story would emphasize video production rather than a novel browser-agent contract.
- The scope contains too many legacy and runtime surfaces for a bounded hackathon proof.

## What remains valuable

- deterministic job lifecycle;
- loopback security boundary;
- artifact provenance;
- cancellation and recovery tests;
- agent-loop evaluation work.

The source repository is not copied or modified here. Two documentation snapshots are retained with provenance in `evidence/SOURCE_MANIFEST.md`.
