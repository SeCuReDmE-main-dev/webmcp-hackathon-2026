# Winter Gate — Cover Composition Brief

## Editorial identity

- Title: **Winter Gate**
- Subtitle: **From a Quantum Router to a Browser-Native Human Decision**
- Edition line: **Day 3-4 Field Report**
- Season: **Winter**
- Author: **Jean-Sébastien Beaulieu**

## Visual continuity

Preserve the monumental gate, evidence cube, deep scientific blue, cyan quantum geometry and restrained metallic gold established by the Autumn cover. Transform the environment into Winter: elegant bare branches carrying fine frost, quiet negative space, subtle crystalline particles and reflected cold light. The gate remains powerful and openable, while its structure becomes easier to inspect. Keep the evidence cube as the central symbol of verifiable state.

## New narrative layer

Represent three distinct lines approaching the gate without turning them into human characters:

1. code and contract geometry for Codex;
2. browser diagnostics and spectral traces for Gemini/Antigravity;
3. a deliberate warm-gold authority line for the human decision.

These lines meet in a shared evidence ledger before reaching the gate. The composition should express collaboration with separated responsibilities, deterministic validation and human authority.

## Palette

- deep navy and midnight blue foundation;
- ice blue, pale cyan and silver for Winter;
- restrained cold gold for authority and continuity;
- white frost highlights;
- avoid dominant purple, generic neon cyberpunk and warm Autumn foliage.

## Scientific boundaries

Use abstract quantum notation, circuit geometry, provenance lines and browser-panel structures. Avoid imagery implying a live QPU connection, guaranteed quantum advantage, provider execution, cryptographic certification or autonomous authorization. The emotional tone is disciplined, visionary, calm and intensely purposeful.
