Evidence state: confirmed from official product and maintainer sources.

## 14:30 — I corrected the room-temperature shorthand

The Shunkai milestone changes an engineering assumption while preserving an essential physics distinction. Its apparatus operates at room temperature without a cryogenic refrigerator, while optical tweezers hold ultracold neutral atoms as qubits.

Evidence state: corroborated by IMS, JST and peer-reviewed neutral-atom research.
Editorial boundary: I will write “room-temperature apparatus with ultracold atomic qubits.” I will exclude any claim that the atoms themselves operate warm or without cooling.
