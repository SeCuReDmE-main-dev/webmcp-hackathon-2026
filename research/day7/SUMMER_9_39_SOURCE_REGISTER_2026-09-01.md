## Evidence boundaries

- Official documentation, standards, original research and institutional
  guidance take precedence.
- The four Kulikowski sources are maintainer-authored perspectives and
  demonstrations. They are not treated as universal standards.
- Professor Aguilar's public profile establishes authorship context only; his
  scientific work is not QCG technical evidence.
- Personal motivation is first-person testimony rather than generalized
  research evidence.
- Webinars, private correspondence and social posts are excluded as technical
  proof.
