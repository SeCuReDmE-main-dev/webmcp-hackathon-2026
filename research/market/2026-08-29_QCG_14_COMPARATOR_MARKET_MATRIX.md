# WebMCP-QCG — 14-comparator market matrix

Date: 2026-08-29
Scope: quantum preflight, validation, estimation, orchestration, provenance and browser/agent access
Decision use: distinguish an executable hackathon contribution from a broad platform claim

## Evidence labels

- **Confirmed by primary sources**: local repository truth, official documentation, official repositories or official pricing.
- **Inferred from multiple sources**: a comparative conclusion produced from more than one primary source.
- **Tentative**: evidence remains missing, conflicting or too early for a product claim.

## Actual job to be done

Before a quantum execution, inspect an experiment, its existing evidence, its requested target and its bounds; then return exactly one decision—reuse, reject, recompile, simulate first or ready—while preserving separate human authority and exporting a portable evidence receipt.

## Market finding

QCG **recombines existing jobs** rather than opening an empty category. Transpilation, resource estimation, spend caps, orchestration, local simulation and provenance all have mature implementations. qBraid and Qiskit MCP also establish that agentic quantum execution already exists.

The narrow gap remains defensible:

> A provider-neutral, browser-native, executionless evidence firewall that decides whether an external quantum call is justified, keeps readiness separate from authorization, and exports a portable receipt before credentials, upload or provider submission.

## Current prototype relevance boundary

Local repository evidence confirms five deterministic cards, four WebMCP tools, one fixed Bell-pair Q# Worker path, visible one-time consent and zero external provider calls. This is a valid feasibility proof. Professional utility begins when the workbench accepts a real artifact, imports a dated target profile, checks a strict evidence-reuse key and records known, stale or unknown cost facts.
